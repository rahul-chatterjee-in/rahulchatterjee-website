EmoShift at Work

Emotional intelligence for managers handling pressure, people and competing priorities.

Practical corporate workshops from twenty years of building capability in BFSI and frontline organisations. Managers leave with tools they use the same week - not binders they shelve.

The flagship workshop

The Pressure, Priorities and People Lab

A 3.5-hour working session for corporate managers. No lectures about feelings - a lab where managers work on their own real week, their own team, their own pressure points.

See yourself under overload

Managers recognise how they actually behave when the week collapses - and what that does to the people watching them.

Manage competing priorities

Deliberate attention-switching instead of reactive multitasking. What gets your best hour, and what gets your leftovers.

Stay emotionally intelligent when it counts

Feedback, delegation and conflict - the three moments where managers lose people, practised live.

Leave with a 14-day plan

Every participant walks out with a personal two-week action plan, not a certificate and a selfie.

Built for: BFSI, NBFC, microfinance, insurance and other organisations with frontline and branch teams.
